Train like an athlete.

Five lifts, one conditioning block, two yoga sessions, one game. Built for upper-body emphasis without trashing your legs before Saturday.

A few principles.

  • Progress matters more than the specific exercises — add a rep or a pound every week on the main lifts.
  • Mornings = stiff body. The warm-up isn't optional, especially before pressing or pulling heavy.
  • Soccer is conditioning. Don't add more on top of Wed's circuit and Saturday's game.
  • If you feel beat up Thursday, swap with Friday — the plan serves you, not the other way around.
  • Sleep and protein move the needle as much as the workout.
